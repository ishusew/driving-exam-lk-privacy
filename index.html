Privacy Policy

Driving Exam LK · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Driving Exam LK ("the app") is a practice app for the Sri Lanka driving licence written exam. It is made by an independent developer (Ish). This page explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address or any account. The app has no sign-up or login.

The app saves these things only on your device: your chosen language, your best mock exam score, the number of exams taken, and the questions you answered wrongly. This data never leaves your phone, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. Google may use this to show personalised ads, depending on your consent and device settings.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is meant for people preparing for a driving licence, who must be at least 18. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share any personal information ourselves. The only third party that receives data is Google AdMob, as described above.

Exam content

Questions are for practice only. The app is not connected to, or approved by, the Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) of Sri Lanka. Always study the official DMT material.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
